# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality / QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, validation plans, and release gates so that product outcomes are not only delivered on time but also meet business and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define the testing strategy for features, integrations, and regressions
- Clarify acceptance criteria and support alignment across product, engineering, and stakeholders
- Coordinate release readiness reviews with engineering and project leads
- Track defects, quality trends, and supporting evidence for sign-off
- Partner with developers on test coverage, automation, and defect prioritization

### Goals
- Improve product reliability and user confidence
- Reduce rework and late-stage surprises
- Ensure quality decisions are visible and data-informed

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, defect triage, and release-readiness check-ins
- Collaboration with Developers during sprint reviews and validation cycles
- Escalation updates to PMs and Product Managers when quality risks affect milestones

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to review testing gaps and defect severity
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are measurable and user-centered
- Supports Project Managers by providing quality risk visibility and readiness signals for milestone decisions

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers ensure that project decisions, design choices, and delivery practices account for secure implementation, risk reduction, and compliance needs.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, data handling, and integrations for security risks
- Participate in threat modeling and secure design reviews
- Define security requirements and guardrails for features and releases
- Coordinate with engineering on vulnerability remediation and secure release checks
- Support PM and Product stakeholders with security risk and compliance implications

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across project delivery
- Secure critical workflows without slowing customer value delivery
- Make risk decisions explicit and actionable across the team

### Typical Communication
- Design review feedback, threat model sessions, and remediation tracking
- Product and engineering discussions about security constraints or trade-offs
- Escalation to PMs and leadership when security issues affect scope, timelines, or release approval

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers to make security requirements practical and testable
- Advises Product Managers on risk trade-offs and compliance considerations
- Works with Project Managers to assess impact on schedule, dependencies, and deployment approval

---

## Release Manager / Deployment Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the sequencing, readiness, and communication needed for successful deployments and rollbacks across project milestones.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare release timelines, deployment windows, and rollback plans
- Validate release readiness against CI, QA, and stakeholder commitments
- Coordinate communications with engineering, support, and leadership before and after launch
- Monitor deployment risk and ensure post-release verification happens
- Manage escalation paths when release quality or operational impact requires intervention

### Goals
- Reduce launch risk and customer disruption
- Ensure predictable, well-communicated delivery outcomes
- Align deployment practices with project governance and business readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists, readiness reviews, launch communications, and incident coordination
- Cross-functional updates with PMs, Developers, and support teams
- Stakeholder notifications when production-impacting changes are scheduled or delayed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to confirm deployable artifacts and operational readiness
- Coordinates with QA Leads on release gates and validation evidence
- Keeps Project Managers and Product Managers informed of milestone risks and go/no-go decisions

---

## Technical Writer / Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Technical Writers and Knowledge Managers keep project content, documentation, and process artifacts current so teams can learn from prior decisions and onboard quickly.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain process and project documentation in the repo, project board, or shared docs
- Update onboarding materials, release notes, and operational guidance as work evolves
- Capture decisions, trade-offs, and lessons learned in accessible written form
- Support consistency between project artifacts and the established process docs
- Help ensure project knowledge does not remain dependent on a single individual

### Goals
- Improve clarity, continuity, and institutional memory
- Reduce friction for onboarding and handoff across teams
- Support repeatable execution based on shared documentation

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews, contribution updates, and process refinement discussions
- Collaboration with PMs and Product Managers on status notes and milestone summaries
- Working sessions with Developers to ensure technical detail is accurately captured

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by maintaining accurate status and process information
- Partners with Product Managers to document requirements, outcomes, and stakeholder context
- Helps Developers maintain clear implementation notes and operational guidance

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Stakeholder Liaison roles connect project delivery to business and user feedback so that roadmap decisions and implementation trade-offs reflect lived customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder and customer feedback on value, adoption, and pain points
- Surface risks, blockers, or opportunities that may not be visible in sprint execution alone
- Help translate stakeholder concerns into actionable product or delivery priorities
- Support communication between product teams and customer-facing groups
- Provide context for release messaging and milestone readiness

### Goals
- Improve customer alignment and adoption outcomes
- Ensure product delivery remains connected to stakeholder needs
- Reduce avoidable churn or confusion caused by poor communication

### Typical Communication
- User feedback summaries, stakeholder briefings, and adoption risk reporting
- Weekly updates with Product Managers and PMs
- Escalation of customer-impacting concerns that require product or roadmap decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds customer insights back to Product Managers for prioritization and planning
- Works with Project Managers to align stakeholder communications and escalation paths
- Collaborates with Developers and QA Leads when user impact needs validation or release monitoring

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help clarify accountability, cross-functional handoffs, and decision ownership across planning, delivery, quality, security, release, and stakeholder communication.
